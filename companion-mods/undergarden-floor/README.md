# Undergarden Floor

The jar is not shipped. Its chunk-load scan deadlocked C2ME while a chunk generated. The source stays here so the old converter can be read.

New chunks get an obsidian floor and an obsidian ceiling from the Paxi datapack `Autisnautics Undergarden Floor`. That datapack overrides `undergarden:undergarden` noise settings: the `minecraft:bedrock_floor` band, the `minecraft:bedrock_roof` band, and the depthrock fill on the top of the world are `minecraft:obsidian`.
